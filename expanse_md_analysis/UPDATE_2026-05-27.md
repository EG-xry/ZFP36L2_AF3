## 2. Why 139 ns is the new common window

`D210E` and `T187S` have the shortest DCD files (1 390 frames × 100 ps =
139.0 ns each).  Every other variant has ≥220 ns.  So **139 ns is the
largest common window that includes every variant honestly**.

The previous 50 ns analysis was just a conservative truncation of all
trajectories to 50 ns; the **139 ns analysis uses 2.78× more frames** for
every variant in the common window.

+ AF3 modality combination would be ≥0.94 trivially.
